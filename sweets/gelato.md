## Gelato

This is an easy recipe for gelato or ice cream

### Before you start

You will need:
* A stand mixer
* An ice cream maker


### Ingredients

* Base
  * 300ml creme fraiche
  * 300ml whole milk
  * 300ml single cream
  * 397g sweetened condensed milk
  * 100g sugar granulated sugar.
  * Flavouring (e.g. Vanilla)
* Chunks (things like choc-chips, or diced Reesed Pieces, etc.)

### Instructions

1. Start pre-cooling the ice creme maker. (If your ice cream maker requires the bowl to be frozen in advance then follow the manufacturers instructions)
2. In the stand mixer pour in the single cream, creme fraiche, whole mild and condensed milk and mix together
3. Add flavouring (e.g. Vanilla)
3. Add the sugar.
4. Mix well.
5. Set the ice cream maker on its make ice cream/mix setting, and pour in the mix to the limit set by the manufacturer to create a batch. (If you have mix left over we'll use it later)
6. Allow the ice cream maker to freeze & mix the batch according to the manufacturers instructions.
7. If you have chunks to add, add them towards the end of the freeze/mix cycle.
8. Spoon the batch into individual ice cream pots or a tub.
9. If you have any mix left from earlier, use that mix and go back to step 5 and repeat from there.

### Notes

**Milk**: Full fat/whole milk is preferred, it makes it easier to work with.

# Banana Muffins

## Before you start

You will need:
* an oven
* a large mixing bowl
* a fork
* kitchen scales
* 20 cupcake tins
* baking tray.

## Ingredients

* 200g Self raising flour
* ¼ tsp Bicarbonate of soda
* ½ tsp salt
* 50g butter
* 175g caster sugar
* 2 large eggs
* 450g peeled bananas (about 5 medium-large)
* 75g chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. pre-heat the oven to 180°C
2. Mash the bananas & butter into the bowl with a fork
3. Add the flour, bicarb', salt, sugar and eggs, and mix until smooth
4. Add the chocolate chips to the mix, and stir in.
5. spoon the mix into the cupcake tins, so each is about half full.
6. Put the cupcakes on a baking tray, or similar, and cook in the oven for 30 minutes.
